Understanding iMessage Number Types

Open Source

Run Photon on your own Mac and it automatically uses your iCloud account as your agent's iMessage number. Perfect for exploring and building locally before going production.

Managed Shared (Free / Pro)

Photon maintains a large pool of numbers and assigns each of your users a fresh one they have never received a message from before. Everything is handled for you with no infrastructure to worry about.

Dedicated Number (Business)

Numbers allocated just for your project. All your users text the same number and get connected to your agent. Nothing is shared with other tenants, giving you full control over your messaging identity.
